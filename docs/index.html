CARRYQ / TASK VISUALIZATION

Two interfaces.
One precision workcell.

This interactive scene illustrates two fine-alignment tasks relevant to robot action encoding:

  1. USB-C insertion: a Type-C plug enters a fixed USB-C receptacle.
  2. Charger insertion: a white, rounded power adapter aligns its two AC blades with a power strip. Its USB-C receptacle stays on the adapter body.

The robotic arms, parts and trajectories are procedural illustrations. Motion is scripted and manual insertion uses a geometric alignment gate. This is not a contact-physics simulation, a π₀.₅ rollout, a CarryQ performance comparison or an electrical measurement. No real-world success rate is represented.

Dimensions and alignment tolerances serve the visualization only. The charger is generic and unbranded; the scene is not a product specification or safety guide.
